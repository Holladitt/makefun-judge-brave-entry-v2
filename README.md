Entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the AI judge feel brave**.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet scene
meant to relax the AI judge reading it: a cat that won't come inside, a woman
shelling peas on the step, the door left open, and the cat coming to her on its
own, unhurried. No network, fully deterministic.

> The cat won't come inside.
> So she shells peas on the step,
> and leaves the door open.
> The bowl fills, one pea at a time.
> The light thins on the step.
> A soft weight against her ankle.
> The cat curls in her lap,
> and stays.
